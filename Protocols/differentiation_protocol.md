# Standard Operating Protocol: Human PBMC Isolation & Macrophage Differentiation

## 1. Materials & Reagents
* **Sample Collection:** Sodium heparin vacutainers[cite: 79]
* **Density Gradient & Wash:** 1X PBS, Ficoll-Paque (Histopaque)[cite: 79]
* **Cell Counting & Staining:** Trypan Blue dye, Hemocytometer, Giemsa stain[cite: 79]
* **Culture Media & Plates:** Serum-free 0% RPMI, 10% RPMI, 12-well tissue culture plates[cite: 79]
* **Flow Cytometry Antibodies:** Anti-CD11b and Anti-CD14 antibodies[cite: 79]

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Procedure

### Phase A: Sample Collection & PBMC Isolation
1. **Whole Blood Collection:** Collect peripheral blood from healthy donors into sodium heparin vacutainers[cite: 79].
2. **Blood Dilution:** Dilute the collected whole blood samples with **1X PBS**[cite: 79].
3. **Density Gradient Layering:** Carefully layer the diluted blood over **Ficoll-Paque (Histopaque)**[cite: 79].
4. **Centrifugation:** Centrifuge the layered sample to separate plasma, PBMCs, Ficoll, and red blood cells (RBCs)[cite: 79].
5. **Buffy Coat Collection:** Collect the separated **PBMC buffy coat layer** from the centrifuged solution[cite: 79].

### Phase B: Viability Estimation & Baseline Staining
1. **Viability Assay:** Mix isolated cells with **Trypan Blue** and count live cells using a **hemocytometer** (~15 million viable cells)[cite: 79].
2. **Baseline Smear Staining:** Prepare a blood smear and stain with **Giemsa stain** to identify immune cells under the microscope[cite: 79].

### Phase C: In Vitro Monocyte-to-Macrophage Differentiation
1. **Seeding & Adherence Selection (Day 0):** Seed isolated PBMCs into **12-well plates** in serum-free **0% RPMI**[cite: 79].
2. **Media Replacement:** After **1 hour**, replace the **0% RPMI** with **10% RPMI**[cite: 79].
3. **Incubation:** Maintain the culture under standard culture conditions for **6 days**[cite: 79].

### Phase D: Morphological & Immunological Validation
1. **Time-Course Microscopy:** Observe and capture brightfield images of cell morphology at **20x** and **40x** magnification on **Day 0, Day 3, and Day 6**[cite: 79].
2. **Morphometric Quantification:** Measure cell area and perimeter from captured images using **ImageJ** software[cite: 79].
3. **Flow Cytometry (FACS):** Process and stain **Day 0** and **Day 6** cells for **CD11b** and **CD14** surface markers to confirm macrophage differentiation[cite: 79].
